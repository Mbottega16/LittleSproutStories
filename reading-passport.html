How this passport works

  1. Read a storybook. Every LittleSprout book is set in a real place in town — a fire station, a bakery, a library, and more.
  2. Find its stamp. Each place on the stamp page matches a badge on the online badge shelf.
  3. Mark it visited. Draw a little stamp, color it in, or add a sticker in the circle, then write the date.
  4. Keep going. Fill all 14 stamps to finish the passport — just like finishing the badge shelf online.

This passport is a free companion to the badge shelf. It isn't required to read any book or earn any online badge — it's just a fun, offline way to track the same progress on paper.
