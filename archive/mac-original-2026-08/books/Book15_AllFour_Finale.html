Community Helpers Too

Looking back on all their visits, the four friends realized they’d learned a little bit from every helper they had met.
They remember every visit.
🚒
“I loved being like Alex, brave and ready to help,” said Marcus.
Marcus felt brave like Alex.
📚
“I loved being like Ms. Chen, sharing stories,” said Maya.
Maya felt like Ms. Chen.
🌱
Sophie and James remembered Tom’s patience and Rose’s careful sorting, and every other helper along the way.
Everyone remembers a helper.
“Let’s make our own pretend town at school!” Sophie said, and everyone lit up with the idea.
They plan a pretend town.
Each friend picked the helper role that fit them best — Marcus as firefighter, Maya as librarian, Sophie as gardener, James as officer.
Everyone picks a role.

What is each friend doing?

For one whole day, their classroom became a bustling little town, full of pretend helpers doing real good.
Their classroom is a town.
At the end of the day, they all agreed: everyone can be a helper, in their own way.
Everyone can help.

What did the friends learn?

For Grown-Ups

Feeling/skill: Pride in recognizing personal growth across many experiences.

Ask your child: Which community helper did you like being the most, and why?

Try this: Let your child set up their own pretend-helper day at home.
