Maya & Sophie
Visit the Library

📚
Maya loved the library’s quiet corners. Sophie loved the library too — but sitting still was hard for her.
Maya and Sophie go in.
👩‍📚
Ms. Chen
Ms. Chen welcomed them and invited everyone to story time on the rug.
Ms. Chen reads.
Partway through, Sophie’s legs just had to wiggle. A few kids looked over. Sophie felt her cheeks get hot.
Sophie feels embarrassed.

How does Sophie feel?

“I think I’m bad at library,” Sophie whispered to Maya.
Sophie feels bad.
📖
Maya took her hand and showed her a comic-book display in the corner. “Look — you can read standing up here.”
Maya shows a book.
👩‍📚
Ms. Chen
Ms. Chen came over. “There’s no one right way to enjoy a book,” she said. “Some readers wiggle, some sit still — both are real readers.”
Every reader is different.
Sophie picked a comic and read it standing on her tiptoes, completely happy.
Sophie is a real reader.

What did Sophie learn?

👩‍📚
Ms. Chen
Maya and Sophie checked out their books together and promised to come back next week.
They check out books.

For Grown-Ups

Feeling/skill: Embarrassment eases with self-acceptance and a friend’s help.

Ask your child: Is there a way you like to do something that is different from your friends?

Try this: Let your child choose how they sit and read tonight.

Standards: RL.K.5, RL.K.6
