Sophie & James
Visit the Police Station

Sophie and James couldn’t wait to visit the police station — Sophie wanted to see everything at once!
Sophie and James go in.
👮‍♀️
Jasmine
⭐
Officer Jasmine greeted them with a big smile and a shiny badge. “Welcome, friends!”
Jasmine has a badge.
James pulled out a checklist. “First the police car, then the radio, then the map wall,” he said.
James has a list.
🚔
But Sophie was already at the police car, pressing every button she could find! James felt his plan slipping away.
James feels frustrated.

How does James feel?

👮‍♀️
Jasmine
“My list is getting all mixed up,” James said quietly. Jasmine knelt down. “Plans can bend a little and still work.”
The list can bend.
🗺️
Jasmine showed them the real patrol schedule for the day. “You can each pick one thing you want to see most.”
Pick one thing.
🚨
Sophie picked the siren button. James picked the map wall. Both got exactly what they wanted.
Sophie and James are happy.

What helped fix the problem?

👮‍♀️
Jasmine
They waved goodbye to Jasmine, promising to be careful crossing streets just like she showed them.
Bye, Jasmine!

For Grown-Ups

Feeling/skill: Frustration eases when a plan is allowed to flex.

Ask your child: What do you do when your plan does not go the way you hoped?

Try this: Let your child help set tonight’s plan, then bend it together if something changes.

Standards: RI.K.3, W.K.2
