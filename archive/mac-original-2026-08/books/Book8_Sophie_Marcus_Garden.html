Little Sprout Stories

Sophie & Marcus
Visit the Garden

🌼
Sophie ran straight for the flower beds, ready to pick every bloom she saw.
Sophie wants flowers.
🤵
Tom
Tom the gardener gently stopped her. “Some flowers are ready, and some need more time,” he explained.
Some flowers wait.
🌱
“But I want one now!” Sophie said, reaching for a tightly closed bud.
Sophie feels impatient.

How does Sophie feel?

Marcus knelt beside a fully open flower nearby. “This one looks ready to me,” he said calmly.
Marcus looks closely.
🤵
Tom
🌷
Tom showed Sophie how to tell — soft, open petals mean ready; tight, green buds mean “not yet.”
Open petals mean ready.
🌼
Sophie searched and searched, and then she spotted one bright, wide-open flower of her very own.
Sophie finds a ready flower.
She picked it carefully, and it felt even better than if she’d grabbed the very first one.
Sophie is glad she waited.

What did Sophie learn?

Marcus and Sophie carried their flowers home, already planning to visit the garden again.
They carry their flowers.

For Grown-Ups

Feeling/skill: Impatience eases with the satisfaction of the right timing.

Ask your child: What is something worth waiting for?

Try this: Plant a seed together and check on it over several days.

Standards: L.K.5.a, L.K.5.c
