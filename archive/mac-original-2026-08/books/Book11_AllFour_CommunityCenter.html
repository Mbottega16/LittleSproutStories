All four friends came to the community center for a favorite group game.
Four friends play a game.

Nurse Aisha
Nurse Aisha explained the rules — everyone would get a turn, one at a time.
Everyone gets a turn.
James went first, and it was hard for Sophie to wait for her turn to come.
Sophie feels impatient.

How does Sophie feel waiting?

“It feels like forever,” Sophie said, bouncing on her toes.
Waiting feels long.
👩‍⚕️
Nurse Aisha
⌛
Nurse Aisha brought out a big sand timer everyone could watch trickle down together.
The sand timer trickles.
⌛
Now Sophie could see exactly how long her wait would be, and watching the sand helped the time pass.
Sophie watches the timer.
When her turn finally came, Sophie played her best round yet.
Sophie takes her turn.

What helped Sophie wait?

All four friends cheered for each other’s turns, win or not.

For Grown-Ups

Feeling/skill: Impatience eases when waiting is made visible and fair.

Ask your child: What helps you wait for your turn?

Try this: Use a visible timer for turn-taking at home this week.
