Marcus & James
Revisit the Fire Station

🚒
Marcus was excited to bring James to the fire station for the very first time.
Marcus brings James.
🚒‍
Alex
Alex remembered Marcus right away. “Welcome back!” she said warmly.
Alex remembers Marcus.
When Alex mentioned the alarm demo, James’s tummy went jumpy — just like Marcus’s had, long ago.
James feels nervous.

How does James feel?

Marcus recognized the look right away. “I felt like that my first time too,” he said.
Marcus understands.
“Want to try the trick that helped me? Cover your ears, and we count to three together.”
Cover your ears. Count to three.
🔔
Alex counted, “One, two, three!” The alarm rang, and James kept his hands steady over his ears.
James is steady.
“That wasn’t so bad,” James said, echoing Marcus’s very first words.
James is brave.

Who helped James feel brave?

🚒‍
Alex
Marcus beamed with pride — being the helper felt just as good as being helped.
Marcus feels proud too.

For Grown-Ups

Feeling/skill: Passing a coping trick to a friend builds confidence in both children.

Ask your child: Have you ever helped a friend the way someone once helped you?

Try this: Ask your child to teach a younger sibling one calming trick.
